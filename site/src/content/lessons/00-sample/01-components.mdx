## Soft targets in one paragraph

A hard label tells the student only that the answer is "mat". The teacher's probabilities say more: "floor" and "rug" are reasonable, "banana" is not. Hinton called this <mark>dark knowledge</mark>, and it is most of what the student learns from.

## Predict first

Students commit to a guess before they see the result. Being wrong first makes the explanation stick.

<PredictReveal
  client:visible
  id="banana"
  prompt="A teacher gives “banana” almost no probability for “The cat sat on the ____”. If you raise the temperature from 1 to 8, what happens to it?"
  options={["It stays near 0%", "It rises, but stays the least likely", "It overtakes “roof”"]}
>
  It rises from about 0.002% to roughly 7%, but it stays the least likely word. Dividing every score by the same temperature changes how confident the teacher looks. It cannot change the order.
</PredictReveal>

## Spaced review
