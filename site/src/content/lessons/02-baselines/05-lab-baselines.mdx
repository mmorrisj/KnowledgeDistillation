This lab produces the numbers the rest of the course is measured against. Nothing is distilled yet.

## What the lab does

1. **Builds the four piles** of data from lesson 2.1, the same for every student.
2. **Scores the teacher.** A 7 billion parameter model reads the list of 77 types and each test message, and replies with a type. It is not trained.
3. **Trains and scores the baseline.** A small encoder with about 150 million weights learns from the 616 gold messages.
4. **Trains and scores the reference.** The same encoder learns from every label, to show what labelling everything would buy.
5. **Compares** the teacher and the baseline with a paired test.

Every score is saved in a `results` folder next to the notebook. Later labs read from it, so keep it.

## Before you look at the results

Write down your prediction: which of the three will score highest, and which lowest? Lesson 2.1 gave you a hint for one of them. Committing to a guess first is what makes the result stick.

## Reading the results

The table at the end shows accuracy with its interval, and macro-F1, for each model. Three things to look for:

- **The order.** If the reference comes out on top, you have seen for yourself that plenty of good labels beat a prompt.
- **The gap between the teacher and the baseline.** This is the room distillation has to work in. The paired comparison tells you whether it is real.
- **The teacher's unusable replies.** The teacher's results include the share of replies that named no valid type. A high share means the prompt, not the model, is holding the score down.

If the teacher does not beat the baseline, that is a finding, not a failure. It would mean this teacher, prompted this way, has nothing to teach this student, and the next step would be a better prompt or a stronger teacher. Module 4 covers both.

## If something goes wrong

<div class="table-wrap">

| What you see | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Out of memory while training | The teacher is still on the GPU | Run the cell that deletes the teacher, then run the training cell again |
| The dataset will not download | The machine cannot reach Hugging Face | Set `KD_COURSE_DATA_DIR` as described in `labs/README.md` |
| Baseline accuracy near 1% | Training did not learn; with 77 types, 1.3% is chance | Check that the training cell ran to the end and the loss went down |
| Almost every teacher reply is unusable | The replies are being cut short or are in an unexpected form | Print a few raw replies and compare them with the list of types |

</div>
